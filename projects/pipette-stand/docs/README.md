# Pipette / Tip Stand

A beginner-friendly bench organization hyperobject for liquid handling.

## Parts / Modos

| Mode | Part | What it is |
|------|------|------------|
| `carousel` | Pipette Carousel | Round stand cradling N single-channel pipettes upright, each with a finger-relief slot. |
| `tip_rack` | Pipette-Tip Rack | Tray with a grid of tip holes on the true 9 mm SBS/ANSI-SLAS pitch. |
| `wall_hook` | Wall Pipette Hook | Wall plate with a keyhole slot and a C-cradle arm holding one pipette by its body. |

## Standards & dimensions

- **Pipette shaft:** 6.0 mm (20–1000 µL) / 3.8 mm (2–10 µL) single-channel — the body is cradled, not the shaft.
- **Tip grid:** 9 mm well pitch (ANSI-SLAS / SBS microplate standard), Ø6.4 mm reference well.
- **Cradle clearance:** +0.6 mm radial around the pipette body.

## Parameters

- `pipette_slots` (2–8) — number of carousel cradles.
- `body_diameter` (14–30 mm) — widest pipette body diameter to hold.
- `tip_cols`, `tip_rows` (2–12 / 2–8) — tip-rack grid size.
- `tip_hole` (3–9 mm) — tip seating hole; ~6.5 mm suits common 200 µL tips.

## Printing notes / Notas de impresión

**EN:** All parts print flat with no supports. The carousel and tip rack are recycled-filament-friendly. Note that FDM PLA/PETG parts are not autoclavable — keep them for dry benchtop organization.

**ES:** Todas las piezas se imprimen planas sin soportes. El carrusel y la gradilla admiten filamento reciclado. Las piezas FDM de PLA/PETG no son autoclavables; úsalas solo para organización en seco.
