# Beaker / Flask Clamp

A lab-glassware support hyperobject sized to real borosilicate flasks, beakers, and 12 mm ring-stand rods.

## Parts / Modos

| Mode | Part | What it is |
|------|------|------------|
| `ring_clamp` | Rod-Mounted C-Ring Clamp | Open C-ring that hugs a flask/beaker body and clamps onto a ring-stand rod. |
| `neck_holder` | Inverted-Neck Drying Holder | Raised plate with keyed neck cutouts so flasks drain/dry inverted. |
| `rod_stand_base` | Weighted Rod-Stand Base | Heavy footed base with a rear vertical rod socket and underside weight pockets. |

## Standards & dimensions

- **Erlenmeyer body Ø:** 125 mL ~70 mm, 250 mL ~84 mm, 500 mL ~108 mm.
- **Erlenmeyer neck OD:** 34 mm (250/500 mL), ~30 mm (125 mL).
- **Griffin beaker 250 mL:** OD ~70 mm × H 95 mm.
- **Support rod:** 12 mm dia (10×1.5 thread into base) / 1/2 in = 12.7 mm.

## Parameters

- `vessel_diameter` (40–120 mm) — flask/beaker body the C-ring hugs.
- `rod_diameter` (8–16 mm) — ring-stand rod, shared by clamp and base.
- `neck_diameter` (20–45 mm) — flask neck OD for the drying holder.
- `neck_holes` (1–6) — drying positions.

## Printing notes / Notas de impresión

**EN:** Print the C-ring flat (mouth to the side); it slips over the vessel and the slit clamp tightens on the rod with a single screw. Use PETG near hotplates for heat/solvent resistance. The base prints upside-down or with light supports under the boss.

**ES:** Imprime el anillo en C plano (con la boca al lado); se desliza sobre el recipiente y la ranura aprieta la varilla con un tornillo. Usa PETG cerca de placas calientes por resistencia al calor/solventes. La base se imprime invertida o con soportes ligeros bajo el saliente.
